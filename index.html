What we do

We get people to tell their story in a way that makes others lean in.

Interviews first. Then the visual work around them — the b-roll that gives a place its texture, cut tight and formatted to match how your brand already speaks.

The part clients mention afterwards, though, is rarely the footage. It's the shoot itself. Production has a reputation for being chaotic and extractive, for treating the people on camera as an obstacle between the crew and the shot. We run it the other way around. The process should feel like a good time, and the people in front of the lens should finish the day glad they said yes.

The test is not whether people watch it. It is whether they send it to someone — and what happens after they do.

  • It recruits Long Beach Unified asking people to come and teach — the district's own recruitment campaign.
  • It raises Five years of event and fundraising films for the Los Angeles Master Chorale.
  • It sells Two television campaigns, and a 40% lift in store traffic.
