Anchor case study · Nonprofit & arts

Five years with the Los Angeles Master Chorale.

From 2018 to 2022 — event films, fundraising pieces, and the Big Sing, which puts thousands of untrained voices in one room and asks them to sing together.

Arts institutions plan on a season calendar, which means every renewal is a deliberate decision made months ahead by people who have already watched last year's film with their board. Being on that calendar five years running is the endorsement.

The run opened with Big Sing California, where as their editing team we delivered exactly what the Chorale wanted to showcase after their statewide event. By Big Sing 2022 our team was carrying the film the whole way — the edit, the master, and delivery. The finished project was ours to land, which is the handoff an institution only makes once it has stopped checking the work.

Case study · Nonprofit & arts

The season a theatre came back.

Altadena Music Theatre is a non-profit that had to rebuild its season after the fire. We made a programme film and two vertical cuts for the company, and they went out while it was asking its community and its funders to help it get back on stage.

They got there. Support and grant funding came in, programming returned, and the season closed with a sold-out run of Little Shop of Horrors. We made the films for that season; the company did the hard part.

The work spanned formats deliberately. A programme film for the website and the funding conversation, and two vertical cuts built for the feeds where a local audience actually finds a show — including the build of Audrey II, the eleven-foot puppet at the centre of the production, and the largest the company has made.
