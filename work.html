Anchor case study · Nonprofit & arts

Five years with the Los Angeles Master Chorale.

From 2018 to 2022 — event films, fundraising pieces, and the Big Sing, which puts thousands of untrained voices in one room and asks them to sing together.

Arts institutions plan on a season calendar, which means every renewal is a deliberate decision made months ahead by people who have already watched last year's film with their board. Being on that calendar five years running is the endorsement.

On Big Sing 2022 our team carried the film all the way through post — the edit, the master, and delivery. The finished project was ours to land, which is the handoff an institution only makes once it has stopped checking the work.
