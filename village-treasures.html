When the work becomes the story

Village Treasures

It started as an assignment. A shop, a client, a shot list. It ended as a film with an original score, two festival selections, and its subject being honored in the chamber of his own city council.

▶

Village Treasures — co-directed with Donovan Gardener

Max ran a shop. That was the brief — go and film the shop.

What the camera found was a man who had spent decades being the reason a neighborhood held together, in the ordinary way that never gets recorded: knowing everyone's name, keeping the lights on, staying when staying stopped making financial sense.

The assignment quietly became something else. David co-directed the film with Donovan Gardener, edited it, and wrote and performed its score — I Call It Kalimba, now on Spotify, Apple Music and Bandcamp.

Selected, and then recognized.

Official Selection at the Pan African Film Festival and the Micheaux Film Festival.

Long Beach City Council screened scenes from the film in chambers and honored Max in person, with formal recognition from Mayor Rex Richardson and Congressman Robert Garcia.

A film about a man who was never the story becoming the reason his city finally said his name out loud.
